2611ECPC302

Control System Engineering

psychology_alt Skill Gap Identified

Students need to move from control theory to selecting, tuning and evaluating controllers for unfamiliar systems under dynamic disturbances and saturation constraints.

task_alt Core Hands-on Activities (5 Units)

1
Controller Selection Challenge

Analyze plant dynamics and select appropriate P/PI/PID or state-feedback control architectures based on transient and steady-state criteria.

2
Stability Diagnosis Challenge

Diagnose closed-loop stability margins (Gain/Phase margin) and identify instability roots on Bode and Nyquist plots for unmodeled dynamics.

3
Disturbance Rejection Challenge

Design feedforward/feedback compensators to reject high-frequency sensor noise and step load disturbances.

4
Controller Tuning Under Constraints

Tune PID parameters under actuator saturation, slew-rate limits, and energy minimization constraints.

5
Robot/Plant Control Mini Challenge

Implement and simulate closed-loop trajectory tracking for an autonomous 2-wheeled robot or inverted pendulum plant in MATLAB/Simulink.

precision_manufacturing
Recommended Platform / Tool MATLAB & Simulink

Control system toolbox, Root Locus design, frequency response analysis, state-space modeling, and non-linear simulation.

2611ECPC303

Analog Integrated Circuits

psychology_alt Skill Gap Identified

Students need to make analog IC design decisions under specifications and competing constraints (Gain vs. Bandwidth vs. Power vs. Noise vs. Voltage Headroom).

task_alt Core Hands-on Activities (5 Units)

1
Analog Architecture Selection Challenge

Evaluate single-stage, cascode, folded-cascode, and two-stage op-amp topologies against target power and gain specifications.

2
Gain-Bandwidth-Power Trade-Off Challenge

Size MOSFET W/L ratios and bias currents to maximize Unity Gain Frequency while maintaining phase margin > 60°.

3
Performance Bottleneck Investigation

Identify circuit non-idealities limiting CMRR, PSRR, and slew rate using AC/Transient small-signal simulations.

4
PVT/Variation-Aware Design Decision

Run Monte Carlo and Process-Voltage-Temperature (PVT) corner sweeps to verify circuit yield under fabrication variations.

5
Analog IC Specification-to-Design Challenge

Design a complete CMOS operational transconductance amplifier (OTA) meeting strict noise, gain, and power limits from scratch in LTspice/Virtuoso.

developer_board
Recommended Platform / Tool LTspice & Cadence Virtuoso

SPICE transistor-level schematic capture, AC/DC/Transient simulation, noise analysis, and layout design.

2611MASJ301

Probability, Statistics and Stochastic Process

psychology_alt Skill Gap Identified

Students need to apply probability and statistics concepts to uncertain, noisy real-world engineering data, random processes, and stochastic decision making.

task_alt Core Hands-on Activities (5 Units)

1
Uncertainty-Based Sensor Decision Challenge

Formulate Bayesian decision rules to fuse multiple noisy sensor readings and minimize false alarm rates.

2
Noisy-Data Probability Challenge

Fit empirical engineering data to Gaussian, Poisson, and Rayleigh distributions using maximum likelihood estimation.

3
Monte-Carlo Engineering Decision Challenge

Simulate system failure probabilities and reliability metrics using 10,000+ Monte Carlo stochastic iterations in Python.

4
Statistical Anomaly Detection Challenge

Compute rolling z-scores, confidence intervals, and hypothesis tests to flag anomalous sensor telemetry in real-time.

5
Timed Probability/Data Challenge

Solve timed competitive problem sets on random variables, correlation, power spectral density, and Markov chains.

code
Recommended Platform / Tool Python, NumPy & MATLAB

Numerical computing, stochastic process modeling, probability distribution fitting, and statistical hypothesis testing.
